## Open your first image {#open}

1. Press **Ctrl+O** and choose an image, or drop an image on the window.
2. Use the wheel to zoom. **Z** or a double-click switches between fit and actual size. Left/Right browses the folder.
3. Press **E** for Retouch. Add an object or paint on a new layer.
4. Use **Save as → CyImgView project (.cyimg)** to keep layers and objects editable.
5. Export a PNG or JPEG under another filename for sharing.

## Move the view safely {#navigation}

In the editor, **hold the right mouse button and drag** to pan. Left dragging uses the active tool: with **V**, it moves an object or layer. These are different actions.

## Start from an empty image {#blank}

**Ctrl+N** opens [document templates](doc:new-document#templates). Choose a size and background, then Create. **Ctrl+Shift+N** adds a painting layer.
