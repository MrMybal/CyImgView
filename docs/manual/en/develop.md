## Adjust your photograph {#adjust}

Select the photograph’s pixel layer, then **Develop**. The right panel includes a histogram and sections for basic tone, curves, HSL, grading, detail, effects and geometry. A retained object must be rasterized before entering a pixel-processing mode.

1. Use Temp/Tint to correct color casts. The eyedropper samples a neutral area.
2. Adjust Exposure, Highlights and Shadows before adding strong Contrast.
3. Double-click a slider to return to its default.
4. Original temporarily shows the source. Before/After splits the view. Undo changes the development settings.

## Reset or finish {#reset}

Use **Reset all** before leaving to discard the adjustments. Returning to Retouch or finishing applies the current settings to the document. After application, Ctrl+Z in Retouch restores the preceding state. A PNG/JPEG does not preserve sliders as a reusable RAW recipe.
