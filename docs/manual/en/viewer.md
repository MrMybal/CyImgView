## Browse and zoom {#navigation}

Left/Right, mouse side buttons and the filmstrip change images. **T** shows or hides the filmstrip. The wheel zooms around the pointer. **0** fits the image, **1** shows it at 100%, and **Z** toggles. At 100%, image pixels align with display pixels.

## Inspect color channels {#channels}

The **RGB** button opens channel previews. **Ctrl+2** returns to RGB; Ctrl+3/4/5/6 selects red/green/blue/alpha. Checkboxes combine channels. Alpha is available on images with transparency; the checkerboard indicates transparent areas.

## Animations and slideshows {#animation}

On animated images, **Space** pauses or resumes; comma and period step backward and forward. **D** starts a folder slideshow; Space pauses it and Up/Down changes the time per image. Escape leaves it. [Supported formats](doc:formats#formats).

## Information and filtering {#information}

**I** opens image information. **P** opens settings. **N** toggles raw pixels, useful for pixel art. **F/F11** toggles fullscreen. [All shortcuts](doc:keyboard-reference#viewer).
